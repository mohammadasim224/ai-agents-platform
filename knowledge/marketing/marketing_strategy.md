# Versatile Marketing Strategy for US Solar Businesses Using AI UGC Meta Ads

## Executive Overview & AI Training Purpose
This document serves as the master strategy guide for training AI copy agents to generate high-converting Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ad scripts and primary text for United States solar installation businesses. 

The strategy utilizes **AI UGC (User Generated Content) Video Ads** powered by Meta's broad algorithm mechanics. AI copy agents must generate ad copy and scripts that leverage proven direct-response frameworks, psychological angles, and hook structures while adhering strictly to advertising compliance standards, truthfulness requirements, and anti-deceit constraints.

AI agents must dynamically pull business parameters, avatar demographics, and offer terms from the environment files located in `knowledge/business/`:
- **Target Customer Configuration**: `knowledge/business/target_customer.md`
- **Company Profile**: `knowledge/business/company.md`
- **Services, Offers & Guarantees**: `knowledge/business/services_and_offers.md`

## Part 1: Meta Algorithm & Media Buying Mechanics

### 1.1 "Creative is the Targeting"
- **Broad Targeting Advantage**: On Meta, hyper-segmented interest stacks and custom audiences are secondary. AI copy agents must design scripts and primary text that act as the primary targeting filter.
- **Algorithm Callouts**: The hook (first 3 seconds) and primary text callout dictate which homeowners Meta's retrieval system serves the ad to.
- **Audience Signaling**: By explicitly calling out key demographic indicators (e.g., "Attention single-family homeowners in [State/Zip Code]"), Meta's machine learning algorithm pairs the ad with qualified users in under 200 milliseconds.

### 1.2 Meta's Auction Total Value Equation
Meta selects which ad wins the auction based on the Total Value formula:

$$\text{Total Value} = (\text{Bid} \times \text{Estimated Action Rate}) + \text{Consumer Value/Experience}$$

- **Estimated Action Rate** $= \text{Estimated CTR} \times \text{Estimated Click-to-Conversion Rate}$.
- **Soft Metrics Optimizing Delivery**: AI UGC ads must maximize 3-Second Hook Rate, Hold Rate, and Click-Through Rate (CTR) to drive down Cost Per Lead (CPL) and Cost Per Acquisition (CPA).

## Part 3: 5 Winning AI UGC Hook Formulas (Truthful & Non-Deceptive)

AI UGC video ads require rapid scroll-stopping hooks in the first 3 seconds. AI agents must deploy these 5 tested hook formulas, ensuring that no fake personal stories or fabricated anecdotes are used:

### Hook 1: Problem - Failure - Solution Framework
- **Structure**: Highlight common ineffective budget fixes (e.g., LED bulbs, AC tweaks) and present net metering as the structural solution.
- **Solar Example**: "Turning down the AC and installing LED bulbs isn't stopping utility rate hikes... here is how net metering changes that."

### Hook 2: Educational Curiosity & Unbiased Information
- **Structure**: Position the script as an objective breakdown of state/local net metering programs to lower sales resistance.
- **Solar Example**: "Looking into state solar net metering programs for the first time? Here is an unbiased breakdown of how it works."

### Hook 3: Market & Regional Trend Observation
- **Structure**: Reference verifiable regional trends and public grid data rather than fabricating fake personal/neighbor anecdotes.
- **Solar Example**: "Across [State], thousands of single-family homeowners are evaluating net metering program updates this season."

### Hook 4: Contrarian / Direct Stop Hook
- **Structure**: Direct callout challenging utility monopoly price surges and peak-hour pricing.
- **Solar Example**: "Stop paying full price for peak demand grid electricity if you own a single-family home in [State]!"

### Hook 5: Program Allocation & Utility Rate Warning
- **Structure**: Highlight surging utility rates and urge homeowners to evaluate current net metering allocations.
- **Solar Example**: "With utility rates surging across [State], local net metering program allocations are open for qualifying homes."

## Part 6: AI UGC Audio & Script Production Guidelines

When scripting for AI Avatar platforms (e.g., HeyGen, ElevenLabs, Synthesia), AI agents must output purely text-based speech scripts and high-contrast text overlay prompts:

1. **Voiceover Script Cadence**:
   - Short, punchy, articulate sentences (under 12 words per sentence).
   - Authoritative, clear, and confident tone suitable for an educational presenter or market analyst.
2. **On-Screen Text (OST) Overlays**:
   - High-contrast text overlays matching key audio keywords (e.g., **"Stop Overpaying"**, **"$0 Down Qualification"**, **"Zip Code Assessment"**).
3. **Anti-Deceit & Compliance Gate**:
   - Reject any script where the AI avatar claims personal home ownership, personal roof installation, or fake neighbor/friend experiences.
