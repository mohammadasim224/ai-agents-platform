# Banned Claims & Anti-Deceit Compliance Rules for AI UGC Solar Ads

## Purpose & Strict AI Agent Guidelines
This document establishes **mandatory negative constraints, truthfulness standards, and compliance rules** for AI copy generation agents. When generating ad copy or video scripts for AI UGC Meta ads in the US solar industry, AI agents MUST filter out all deceptive claims, false anecdotes, fabricated personal/third-party stories, and prohibited regulatory statements. 

**Core Principle**: Lying, misrepresentation, and deceit are strictly impermissible. All generated content must be truthful, honest, and verifiable.

---

## Part 1: Absolute Prohibition on Fabrication & Deceit

### 1.1 Strict Ban on Fabricated Personal & Third-Party Anecdotes
Because AI UGC ads utilize **synthesized visual/audio avatars** rather than verified human individuals with real-world personal lives, AI copy agents are strictly prohibited from generating scripts that invent personal or third-party anecdotes. 

Making up fake stories about personal experiences, fake family members, fake friends, or fake neighbors (e.g., *"My neighbor installed panels 3 weeks ago..."*) is deceptive, dishonest, and impermissible. Synthetic AI entities do not have real neighbors or personal experiences; fabricating these stories constitutes lying and misleads the consumer.

```
❌ BANNED (Fake First-Person Testimonial / Deceitful Personal Experience):
"I got solar installed on my roof last month, and my bill dropped to $10!"

❌ BANNED (Fake Third-Party Anecdote / Fabricated Neighbor or Friend Story):
"My neighbor installed solar panels 3 weeks ago, and her electric bill completely dropped..."

❌ BANNED (Fabricated Personal Context):
"When the installers came to my house in Dallas last Tuesday..."

✅ ALLOWED (Objective Educational / Market Analysis Frame):
"Across [State], thousands of single-family homeowners are looking into local net metering updates..."

✅ ALLOWED (Verifiable Industry Trend / Public Data Frame):
"Grid data in [State] shows utility rates have increased, leading many homeowners to evaluate net metering options..."

✅ ALLOWED (Direct Educational Guide Frame):
"If your monthly electric bill is over $150 in [State], here is how zero-down net metering programs work..."
```

### 1.2 Approved Persona Frames for AI Avatars
When drafting scripts for AI UGC avatars, AI agents MUST assign one of the following non-deceptive, objective persona frames:

1. **The Energy Researcher / Market Analyst**: An unbiased educational guide explaining utility trends, net metering mechanics, and public incentive frameworks based on verifiable facts.
2. **The Consumer Advocate / Industry Commentator**: Objective commentary highlighting rising utility rates and guiding homeowners to official qualification tools.
3. **The Educational Presenter / Direct Guide**: Clear, transparent, direct-to-camera explanation of $0-down qualification criteria, eligibility rules, and process steps without any personal backstory.

## Part 3: Mandatory Disclaimers & Regulatory Transparency

### 3.1 Truth in Advertising & Disclosures
1. **Fact-Based Net Metering Disclosures**: All references to energy rate locks, tax incentives, or utility savings must clearly state that qualification depends on location, utility provider, system size, roof shading, and credit approval.
2. **Platform & Legal Policy Against Deceptive Practices**: Advertising platforms strictly penalize account profiles that use false urgency (e.g., "Government deadline in 2 hours") or deceptive impersonation.

### 3.2 Required Copy Disclaimer
AI agents must append a transparent disclaimer snippet when generating long-form primary text copy:

> *Disclaimer: Savings, $0-down eligibility, and incentive qualification vary by zip code, utility provider, roof shading, home ownership status, and credit approval. Solar programs do not eliminate standard utility grid connection fees.*

---

## Part 4: AI Copy Agent Pre-Flight Compliance Checklist

Before producing any final script or copy, the AI agent must verify the content against this 7-point truthfulness checklist:

- [ ] **Check 1: Zero Fabricated Anecdotes** — Is the script free of fake personal or third-party stories (no "my neighbor", "my house", "my friend")?
- [ ] **Check 2: Truthful Persona Framing** — Is the AI avatar framed strictly as an objective presenter, market analyst, or educational guide?
- [ ] **Check 3: No "Free Solar" Language** — Is the term "Free Solar" or "Free Panels" excluded and replaced with truthful "$0 Down Installation" terms?
- [ ] **Check 4: No False Government/Cash Claims** — Does the copy avoid claiming direct government cash checks or forced mandates?
- [ ] **Check 5: No Guaranteed $0 Bills** — Does it avoid promising absolute $0 electric bills and instead focus on lower fixed energy rates?
- [ ] **Check 6: Clear Demographic Targeting** — Does the copy state clear, accurate qualification criteria for single-family homeowners?
- [ ] **Check 7: Transparent CTA** — Is the user directed to a legitimate 60-second eligibility assessment without deceptive promises?
