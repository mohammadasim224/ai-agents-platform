# Services & Offers


## Core Service
### Residential Solar Evaluation
SunPeak Solar provides homeowners with a personalized evaluation of whether a residential solar system may make sense for their property.

The evaluation may consider:
- Current electricity usage
- Utility provider and applicable rate structure
- Home location
- Roof characteristics and shading
- Estimated system size
- Available financing options
- Applicable incentives or tax benefits, subject to eligibility
- Estimated solar production
- Estimated energy offset
- Expected project economics

## Consultation
### Solar Consultation
A consultation is designed to understand the homeowner's current electricity situation, answer questions, and determine whether a solar proposal is appropriate.

The consultation is informational and does not itself guarantee approval, savings, financing, installation, or a particular system design.

## Offer Structure — Sample
### Free Solar Evaluation
**Price:** $0 for the initial evaluation.

The evaluation is intended to help a homeowner understand whether they may qualify for solar and what the potential economics could look like.

**Important:** "Free evaluation" does not mean that solar equipment or installation is free.

## Sample Deliverables
A qualified homeowner may receive:
- A review of current electricity costs
- An estimated system design
- Estimated annual solar production
- Estimated energy offset
- A review of applicable incentives, when eligibility can be verified
- Available financing/payment scenarios, when applicable
- A projected comparison between the homeowner's current electricity costs and the proposed solar scenario

## Pricing & Financing
The exact project price, financing structure, monthly payment, interest rate, term, and other commercial terms depend on the homeowner's situation and the selected project.

Agents must not invent or assume:
- A universal monthly payment
- A universal system price
- A universal interest rate
- A universal loan term
- A universal savings amount
- A guaranteed $0 electric bill

## Incentives
Potential incentives and tax benefits depend on location, utility, project details, ownership status, and applicable rules at the time of the project.

Agents may explain incentives in general terms but must not state that every homeowner qualifies.

## Guarantees
No universal savings guarantee is defined in this sample business data.

Do not promise:
- A specific dollar amount of savings
- A specific percentage reduction in the utility bill
- A $0 electric bill
- Elimination of the utility bill
- Guaranteed approval
- Guaranteed financing
- Guaranteed incentive eligibility

If a real business guarantee is later added, document its exact terms here before agents use it.

## Service Boundaries
The business in this sample is focused on residential solar for homeowners. Do not assume commercial solar, batteries, roofing, HVAC, EV chargers, or other services are offered unless they are explicitly added to this file.

## Claims Agents May Safely Frame as Conditional
Use language such as:
- "may reduce electricity costs"
- "may help protect against future utility-rate increases"
- "depending on your utility and home"
- "if you qualify"
- "based on your usage and property"
- "we can evaluate whether solar makes sense for you"

Avoid turning conditional concepts into guarantees.
