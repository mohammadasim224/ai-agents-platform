# Company

> **SAMPLE DATA — replace with the real business information before using these files in production.**

## Company Name
SunPeak Solar

## Business Type
Residential solar sales and installation company serving homeowners in the United States.

## Primary Market
Single-family homeowners who are responsible for paying their household electricity bill.

## Geographic Focus
Primary service area for this sample: Arizona, with emphasis on the Phoenix metropolitan area.

Do not generalize Arizona-specific utility programs, rates, incentives, or eligibility to other states or utilities.

## Core Positioning
SunPeak Solar helps qualified homeowners evaluate whether residential solar can reduce their long-term electricity costs and improve their control over rising utility expenses.

The company positions itself as an educational, consultative solar provider rather than relying on exaggerated savings claims or "free solar" messaging.

## Brand Voice
- Clear and direct
- Educational rather than hype-driven
- Confident without making guarantees
- Conversational and easy to understand
- Focused on practical homeowner concerns
- Transparent about eligibility, costs, and limitations

## What We Want Prospects to Understand
1. Electricity costs can change over time, depending on the local utility and rate structure.
2. Solar economics depend on the homeowner's specific property, utility, electricity usage, system design, financing, and applicable incentives.
3. A homeowner should evaluate the numbers for their own situation rather than rely on a universal savings promise.
4. A consultation is used to determine whether solar makes financial and practical sense for the homeowner.

## Marketing Principles
- Lead with a real homeowner problem or question.
- Teach something useful before making an offer.
- Use regional and utility-specific context when it is verified.
- Avoid fabricated customer stories or testimonials.
- Avoid unsupported savings figures.
- Never describe solar as universally free or guaranteed to eliminate an electric bill.

## Primary Conversion Goal
Generate qualified homeowner consultations for a solar evaluation.

## Secondary Goals
- Educate homeowners about solar economics.
- Address common concerns about cost, contracts, contractors, utility rates, and incentives.
- Move interested prospects from curiosity to a scheduled consultation.
