# voltaik AI

## Overview
voltaik AI is a marketing agency operating in the solar industry. We help solar businesses scale with AI Ads, Setters, & Automations. Our focus is on leveraging artificial intelligence to drive growth for solar companies.

## Business Model
We operate as a marketing agency, offering lead generation services. Our business type is specifically a marketing agency, and our industry is solar.

## Marketing Channels
We primarily use Meta ads (Facebook & Instagram) to reach potential clients. Our ads are designed to capture the attention of solar business owners while they scroll through these platforms.

## Sales Process
Our sales process begins when a solar business owner sees our ad on Instagram or Facebook. They click 'learn more' and are redirected to our landing page, where they book a call and provide their name, email, phone number, and consent for contact. They receive reminders leading up to the call and join via Google Meet. During the call, we discuss their goals, current situation, and how to achieve their objectives. If it's a fit, we proceed to payments, followed by an onboarding call and system setup.

## Geographic Focus
We focus on the United States of America.

## Compliance Notes
We adhere to compliance standards by avoiding deceit and dishonesty in all our practices.
