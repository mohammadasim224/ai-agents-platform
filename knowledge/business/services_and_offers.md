# Services and Offers

## Services
We sell lead generation services around AI-generated UGC meta ads. Additionally, we provide an AI appointment setter that calls leads within 45 seconds of opting in to book them into the business's calendar. We send automated email, SMS, and voice call reminders to prospects leading up to the call. In the rare occasion that a lead doesn't answer the phone, we have a nurturing sequence to try to convert them. We also optimize sales scripts, including appointment setter scripts and closer scripts, and write ad scripts and ad copy for their ads.

## Offers
Our primary offer is a pay per sit basis, meaning clients only pay after we get them a booked, qualified appointment. We also promise to 2x-3x your close rate with a 45 second speed to lead.

## Pricing
Our pricing is structured on a pay per sit basis—clients only pay after we secure a booked, qualified appointment for them.
