# Outbound Solar Appointment Setting Call Transcript (1-on-1 Call)

**Roleplay Context:** Outbound 1-on-1 appointment setting call placed by a solar appointment setter (Alex) to an opt-in lead (Marcus) who filled out an online form requesting information on lowering electricity bills and residential solar options.

---

### STAGE 1: INTENT & INITIAL GREETING

**Alex (Setter):** *(Casual, confident, fast, sharp tonality - no hesitation)*  
Hey hey, is this Marcus?

**Marcus (Homeowner):**  
Yeah, this is Marcus. Who's this?

**Alex (Setter):**  
Yeah, what's up buddy! It's just Alex here from Voltaik Solar. It looks like you opted in online about possible help with lowering your power bill and getting solar on the home, is that about right?

**Marcus (Homeowner):**  
Oh, yeah, yeah. I remember filling something out a couple of days ago while looking at my last electric bill.

**Alex (Setter):**  
*(Curious tonality, head tilted)*  
Easy man. Well, just to see if I could help, because I might be able to send you like a bit of a more specific breakdown for your home... what was your main intent behind I guess even looking into solar in the first place? What were you hoping to get out of it?

**Marcus (Homeowner):**  
Honestly, I'm just sick of these electric bills spiking every summer. I want to get my monthly costs down and stop throwing money away to the power company.

---

### STAGE 2: LOGICAL CERTAINTY & CURRENT UTILITY PROCESS

**Alex (Setter):**  
*(Curious tonality)*  
Fair enough man. What have you seen that I guess makes you feel like you're paying way too much on your utility bills right now?

**Marcus (Homeowner):**  
Well, last month's bill came in at over $480. During winter it's around $220, but as soon as June hits and the AC turns on, it practically doubles. It's totally unpredictable.

**Alex (Setter):**  
*(Skeptical tonality, scrunched eyebrows)*  
Gotcha. Well, what are you doing now in terms of your power usage that you feel like isn't allowing you to keep those bills down?

**Marcus (Homeowner):**  
I mean, I try turning the thermostat up to 78 when I'm at work, I switched all the lightbulbs to LEDs, but it doesn't seem to make a dent. The rate per kilowatt-hour from the utility company just keeps going up every single year.

**Alex (Setter):**  
*(Curious tonality)*  
And how long have you been dealing with those high rates from the utility company?

**Marcus (Homeowner):**  
I've owned this house for about five years now, and every summer it gets worse.

**Alex (Setter):**  
*(Skeptical tonality)*  
And what caused you to just stay with the utility company in the first place for those five years?

**Marcus (Homeowner):**  
Honestly, I never really thought I had a choice. You buy a house, you pay the power company. That's just what everyone does.

**Alex (Setter):**  
*(Skeptical tonality)*  
Do you like it though? Like, do you like relying on the utility company for all your power?

**Marcus (Homeowner):**  
No, not at all. It feels like renting power with zero control over the landlord.

**Alex (Setter):**  
*(Skeptical tonality)*  
Well, it can't all be terrible man. Like, what do you like about just paying the power company every month?

**Marcus (Homeowner):**  
*(Pauses)*  
I mean... I guess the power stays on and I don't have to think about maintenance. But that's literally it.

**Alex (Setter):**  
*(Curious, casual tonality)*  
Okay, it doesn't sound like terrible then, but is there anything you would change about either the utility company or the monthly rate you get from them if you could though?

**Marcus (Homeowner):**  
I'd lock in a fixed rate so I'm not getting blindsided every July and August. And I'd rather build equity in my own system instead of throwing $5,000 a year down the drain.

**Alex (Setter):**  
*(Curious tonality, probing)*  
What do you mean by that?

**Marcus (Homeowner):**  
Meaning if I spend $400 a month on electricity, over 10 years that's over $48,000 and I have zero assets to show for it. I'm just funding the utility company's profit margin.

**Alex (Setter):**  
*(Curious tonality)*  
And how long has that been going on for to where you're just throwing money at the utility company like that?

**Marcus (Homeowner):**  
Like I said, five full years in this home, but really everywhere I've lived since I became an adult.

**Alex (Setter):**  
*(Concerned/Empathetic tonality, slowed pacing, leaning in)*  
And without assuming anything, you paying those rising electric rates for the past five years, has that had an impact on your monthly budget or peace of mind in any way?

**Marcus (Homeowner):**  
Yeah, definitely. In the summer, that extra $250 a month comes directly out of my savings or vacation fund. It forces me to tighten my belt for three or four months every single year.

---

### STAGE 3: QUALIFICATION & BOOKING THE CLOSER

**Alex (Setter):**  
*(Casual tonality)*  
In an ideal world, Marcus, where do you want to be in six months in terms of your power costs?

**Marcus (Homeowner):**  
I want a predictable monthly rate that's at least 30% lower than my average utility bill, and I want to own my energy production.

**Alex (Setter):**  
*(Curious tonality)*  
Let me ask you, if there was a way to lock in a fixed lower rate and own your power instead of renting it, would you actually be willing to make a change if that's what it took?

**Marcus (Homeowner):**  
Yeah, if the numbers make sense and it actually protects my budget, absolutely.

**Alex (Setter):**  
*(Casual, confident tonality)*  
Okay, easy man. Well based on what I've heard, obviously you mentioning that your electric bills are spiking to nearly $500 in the summer and you're tired of renting your power with nothing to show for it, I feel like there might be a possibility of being able to help you. 

What I can do from here is connect you with Jordan, our senior residential solar specialist. He can dive a lot deeper into your current roof layout, sun exposure, and exact utility usage records. If he feels we can help, he can walk you through what a custom net-metered setup would look like for your property. How does that sound, would that be awful?

**Marcus (Homeowner):**  
No, that sounds fair. I'd be open to taking a look.

**Alex (Setter):**  
*(Casual tonality)*  
Awesome. I've got Jordan's calendar open right here for a 1-on-1 session. Does tomorrow evening at 6:30 PM work best for your schedule to review the numbers?

**Marcus (Homeowner):**  
Tomorrow evening at 6:30 PM works great for me.

**Alex (Setter):**  
*(Confident tonality)*  
Perfect. I've got you locked in 1-on-1 for tomorrow at 6:30 PM. I'm going to text you a quick confirmation link along with a 2-minute overview video on net metering so you can see how homeowners in your area are locking in fixed energy rates. Lock that into your calendar, and Jordan will see you tomorrow at 6:30 PM sharp. Fair enough man?

**Marcus (Homeowner):**  
Sounds great, Alex. Thanks for the call.

**Alex (Setter):**  
*(Casual tonality)*  
Catch you tomorrow, Marcus. Have a good one!
